How this budget is graded

Actual income uses $15 × random 25–35 weekly hours × 4.33, not the student’s income estimate. Rent costs the greater of $1,000 or planned rent. Other essentials cost the greater of $400 or the sum of food, utilities, transportation and personal expenses. Bills come first, then that month’s investment (up to $500), then fun (up to $400). Savings is a cash target, not a separate deduction.

Investments are locked and earn 0.8% monthly. Happiness = 400 × (1 − exp(−actual fun / 150)), minus 50 if the requested fun budget is zero, 500 for unpaid rent, 300 for unpaid other essentials, and 35 per bad-fortune event. Final score = cash + investments + total happiness.
