Build your own budget, one month at a time. Start with $3,000 cash. Estimate your income, itemize your expenses, and decide what to save.

Draft stays in this browser

Everyone starts with $3,000 cash. Each month is prefilled with $1,948.50 expected income ($15/hour × 30 hours/week × 4.33 weeks), $1,000 rent, and $400 essentials: $250 food plus $50 each for utilities, transportation and personal essentials. You can adjust these estimates. Actual work varies from 25–35 hours/week, and events can change income. Choose your own fun, investment and savings allocations.

Month 1

Enter 0 explicitly for categories you do not fund. Savings is a cash allocation, not an expense; investments are locked for the year.

Your year, on paper

Review planned income, spending and cash. These are forecasts, not simulation scores. Savings remains in cash; unassigned dollars stay available. A negative balance needs a funding plan.
