Build your own budget, one month at a time. Start with $3,000 cash. Plan for a range of income, itemize your expenses, and decide what to save.

Draft stays in this browser

Everyone starts with $3,000 cash. Expected monthly income is $1,500–$2,100 ($15/hour × 25–35 hours/week × 4 weeks). The grader randomly generates the actual hours; job loss can reduce wages to $0. Rent starts at $1,000 and essentials at $400: $250 food plus $50 each for utilities, transportation and personal essentials. Choose your own spending and savings allocations.
